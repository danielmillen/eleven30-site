About

I'm [your name] — a solo developer in Florida, 25 years in.

I've spent most of my career building software for other people — a quarter century of platforms, teams and roadmaps I didn't write. Eleven30 is the part I do for myself: a small studio where I get to finish things properly instead of shipping the version that fit the sprint.

Both apps started as small irritations. The tip calculator came from a table, with a pen, doing arithmetic I shouldn't have had to do. The launch app came from missing a launch I could have watched from my own driveway.

Same instinct either way: do the arithmetic for the person, and show your work.

I answer the support email myself. There's no team behind the address.
